---
title: "Apple Pay button missing"
description: "Open Paylands in Safari — never in a WebView or iframe."
---

`POST /v1/teller/order/proforma` with `"method": { "type": "apple-pay" }` returns an `orderId`. Then `POST /v1/teller/order` with that id. Execute **200** is when `provider.requestConfiguration.uri` appears.

Apple does not show Apple Pay inside WebViews or iframes. Open that URI in **Safari / the system browser**. Google Pay and cards may use an in-app WebView.

If the button is missing **even in Safari**, inspect whether `apple_pay.js` loads on Paylands. That is a checkout-template issue, not your HMAC.

Full guide: [Apple Pay and Google Pay](/partner/apple-google-pay).
