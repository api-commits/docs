---
title: "Apple Pay button missing"
description: "Open Paylands in Safari — never in a WebView or iframe."
---

`POST /v1/teller/order/proforma` with `method: "apple-pay"` returning **200** only means the session exists.

Apple does not show Apple Pay inside WebViews or iframes. Open `data.provider.requestConfiguration.uri` in **Safari / the system browser**. Google Pay and cards may use an in-app WebView.

If the button is missing **even in Safari**, inspect whether `apple_pay.js` loads on Paylands. That is a checkout-template issue, not your HMAC.

Full guide: [Apple Pay and Google Pay](/partner/apple-google-pay).
