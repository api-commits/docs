---
title: "Missing subaccount header"
description: "Empty balances and parent-scoped calls when X-SUBACCOUNT-ID is omitted."
---

After `POST /v1/account/subaccount`, every end-user call needs:

```http
X-SUBACCOUNT-ID: {userId}
```

If you omit it, you operate the **parent**. Symptoms: empty pockets, KYC status that does not match the user, withdrawals on the wrong wallet.

Store `userId` (UUID) in your database. Do not send a partner-internal user id.

Related: [Account model](/guides/account-model).
