---
title: "HTTP 429 and 418"
description: "Back off inside Starter 600 / Medium 800 / Pro 1000 RPM."
---

<Info>
Check [status.bit2me.com](https://status.bit2me.com) before opening a ticket. If this persists, [open a support ticket](https://support.bit2me.com).
</Info>

1. Map your tier: 600 / 800 / 1000 requests per minute.
2. Queue on the client. Do not retry in a tight loop.
3. Gateway **notification** WebSocket: 50 connections per IP, 50 messages per second. Those numbers do not apply to Pro Trading WebSocket (command RPM is unpublished).
4. Repeated abuse can become **418** (banned). Stop traffic and contact [support](https://support.bit2me.com).

Tier changes go through commercial/support — not an implicit raise in a ticket reply.
