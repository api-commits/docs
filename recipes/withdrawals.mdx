---
title: "Which withdrawal path?"
description: "Address book vs Wallet legacy, and when Travel Rule applies."
---

| Path | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Address book](/partner/withdrawals-address-book) | Current OpenAPI target: **verify the destination wallet** (create + 2FA confirm) → draft → accept |
| [Wallet legacy](/partner/withdrawals-wallet) | Still live: proforma + execute with `destination.address`. First-use destination confirm is deprecated here |

[Travel Rule](/partner/travel-rule) is `POST /v2/blockchain-manager/travel-rule-order/{id}`, **only** when address-book accept status is `pending-user-information`. It is not Embed `POST /v1/b2m/transaction/report`.

Do not use Travel Rule v1. Do not invent TTLs, sunset dates, or EUR thresholds.
