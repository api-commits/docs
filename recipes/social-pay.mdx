---
title: "Social Pay is not a pocket transfer"
description: "Escrow and claim vs destination.pocket."
---

`operation: social-payment` creates a **Pay** order. Funds can wait in escrow until claim, auto-claim, or expiry refund. That is not an instant treasury post to a pocket.

For known pocket UUIDs, use `destination.pocket` instead. See [Social Pay vs pocket transfer](/partner/transfers).

Do not invent a silent master–sub product or an email-off switch.
