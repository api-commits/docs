## Rules that prevent tickets

- One end user = one subaccount. Send `X-SUBACCOUNT-ID` after create.
- Do not treat the user as live until KYC `status: verified` and `tier: 2` (persons).
- Do not call identity init on `type: company`.
- The first time a subaccount sends crypto to a new blockchain address, [verify that destination](/partner/withdrawals-address-book) with 2FA before you withdraw.
- Travel Rule is [its own gateway call](/partner/travel-rule), only when address-book accept status is `pending-user-information`. Do not use Embed `report` for this.
- Apple Pay must leave the in-app WebView.
