The Partner **REST** tab highlights Embed (`openapi/embed.json`: subaccount, signin, account, B2M authorize/report). Most partner money movement is on the [Wallet REST](/wallet/rest-overview) and Funding tags of `crypto.json`.

| Area | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Widgets / session | `openapi/embed.json` |
| KYC, pockets, teller, withdrawals | `openapi/crypto.json` |

Playground is **simple** because HMAC cannot be computed in the browser. Copy examples, sign on your backend.

Until cutover: [api.bit2me.com/doc](https://api.bit2me.com/doc).
