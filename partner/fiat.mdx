Fiat collection uses the **Funding / Teller** family. Crypto stays in subaccount pockets until you withdraw on-chain.

<Info>
Check [status.bit2me.com](https://status.bit2me.com) before you debug a timeout or 5xx. If the platform is healthy and the error persists, [open a support ticket](https://support.bit2me.com).
</Info>

## Prerequisites

1. Parent KYB complete.
2. Subaccount created and, for persons, KYC `verified` / `tier: 2`.
3. Fiat pocket for the currency.
4. `x-subaccount-id` plus HMAC headers.

## Deposit instructions

```http
GET /v1/teller/pocket/reference
```

Use this for bank-transfer details tied to a pocket (EUR). Listen for `teller.deposit.transfer.success` and `teller-order-v1`.

## Teller proforma

```http
POST /v1/teller/order/proforma
```

Then complete payment at the provider URL. Confirm with `GET /v1/teller/order/status` and WebSocket `teller-order-v1`.

Documented `method` values include `creditCard`, `google-pay`, `apple-pay`, and `hub-latam` (LATAM rails such as PIX). See [Apple Pay and Google Pay](/partner/apple-google-pay).

## Fiat withdrawals

Bank withdrawals are a separate Funding path from crypto withdrawals. Follow the Funding tag in the REST reference. Do not reuse the address-book crypto flow for SEPA/PIX out.

USD availability on Wallet is not documented as generally available — confirm with sales if you need USD.
