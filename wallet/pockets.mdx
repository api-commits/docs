---
title: "Pockets"
description: "Create and list currency pockets under a parent or subaccount wallet."
---

A pocket is an optional sub-ledger per currency (EUR, BTC, ETH, and others). You can also operate at wallet level.

Every call for an end user must include `X-SUBACCOUNT-ID` after you create the subaccount. See [Account model](/guides/account-model).

## List pockets

```http
GET /v1/wallet/pocket
```

## Create a pocket

Create a pocket for the currency you need before you fund, buy, or withdraw that asset.

Exact request fields are in the [REST reference](/wallet/rest-overview). Amounts are decimal **strings**. Use `.` not `,`.

## Typical partner sequence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the subaccount">
    `POST /v1/account/subaccount` → store `userId`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete KYC or KYB">
    Natural persons: [KYC](/partner/kyc) until `verified` and `tier: 2`. Companies: [KYB](/partner/kyb).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create fiat and crypto pockets">
    EUR (or the fiat in your contract) plus the crypto assets you will buy or receive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund, then operate">
    [Fiat funding](/partner/fiat), [buy and sell](/wallet/buy-sell), then [withdrawals](/partner/withdrawals-address-book).
  </Step>
</Steps>
